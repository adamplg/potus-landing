Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

POTUS is an early access waitlist operated by Stratara Ltd, company no. 517197182, Israel. This page explains exactly what we collect when you sign up, why we collect it, and who else can see it. We have tried to keep it plain rather than legalistic.

What we collect

If you submit the signup form, we record:

DataWhy
Your email addressTo notify you when POTUS launches, or to invite you to early access before it does. This is the only thing you actively type.
Approximate country and cityDerived from your IP address, to understand where interest is coming from.
Referring page and UTM campaign tagsTo understand which channels bring people to the site.
Browser language, screen size, and user agentTo understand which devices to prioritise in testing.

If you only browse the site and never submit the form, we do not collect your email address or any of the signup data above.

The call with Mike

If you answer Mike's call on this site, your voice is sent to ElevenLabs, which runs the conversation. ElevenLabs turns what you say into text and passes it to the AI model providers it uses, to write Mike's replies. Mike is an AI voice. We don't record the call. When the call ends, its transcript is deleted from ElevenLabs; if a call is cut off before that, its transcript is removed in a later clean-up.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which sections people engage with. It sets cookies in your browser and reports aggregate usage to Google. We record a sign_up event when the form is submitted successfully, but we do not send your email address to Google Analytics.

You can opt out of Google Analytics across all sites using Google's browser add-on, or by using your browser's tracking protection.

Who else sees your data

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers. We do rely on a small number of service providers to run the site:

  • Google — stores the waitlist (Google Sheets), runs our analytics, and serves the web fonts used on this site.
  • Cloudflare — hosts and delivers the site.
  • ElevenLabs, and the AI model providers it uses — run the voice call with Mike, if you answer it.
  • ipapi.co — converts your IP address into an approximate country and city at the moment you submit the form.

Loading this page also reveals your IP address to Google (for fonts) and Cloudflare (for hosting), as it would on most websites.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until POTUS launches and you have been notified, or until you ask us to delete it — whichever comes first. If the project is discontinued, the waitlist is deleted.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to tell you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it entirely. Depending on where you live, you may have a legal right to these things (for example under the GDPR in the UK and EU, or the CCPA in California); we honour these requests regardless of where you live.

To make a request, write to us through the contact form on stratara.ai, and mention the email address you signed up with.

Children

POTUS is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we will remove the record.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
